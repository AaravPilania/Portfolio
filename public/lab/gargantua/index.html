Gargantua

100 million solar masses. Spin 0.999. Ten billion light years from home.

01

Endurance. Twelve modules, one hub, a Ranger on the spine. Built here from boxes and maths, lit only by the disk.

02

Approach. Every pixel is a photon traced backwards through curved spacetime. The disk's far side is bent over the shadow and under it.

03

Beaming. The side of the disk turning toward us burns brighter. Close to the horizon, light climbs out redder and dimmer.

04

Horizon. No signal from here on.

05

Cooper. Move the cursor. He drifts with it.

06

Tesseract. Time laid out as a place. Every thread is a moment of one room.
