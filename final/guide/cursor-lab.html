[ Note 03 ]

A cursor is the only part of a website that is always touching the visitor. It should behave like a good pen: quiet while you read, exact while you aim, a little theatrical when you commit.

Try selecting this sentence. The pointer narrows to an I-beam, the tag steps aside, and every study keeps the same bright white dot at its centre, so the site never loses its signature. Only the ink around it changes.
