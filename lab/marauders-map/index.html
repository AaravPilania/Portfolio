The Marauder’s Map

An interactive lab piece. The parchment is blank until you type “I solemnly swear that I am up to no good”, or tap it three times. Ink then draws Hogwarts from the point of your wand, the map unfolds, and named footprints wander the corridors. Hover a name for a remark from Messrs Moony, Wormtail, Padfoot and Prongs. Type “mischief managed” to close it.
